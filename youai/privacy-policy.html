Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YOU.AI (hereinafter referred to as the "App", "Service", or "we") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our AI-powered photo and video generation application.

Quick Summary: We respect your privacy. We do not collect personal identifiers like your name or email. We use AI (via fal.ai) to process images. Any face data used during processing is transient, never stored permanently, and never used for user identification.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials or authentication data
  • Your generated photos and videos (after delivery to your device)
  • Biometric identifiers used to identify a specific individual
  • Location data (precise location)
  • Contact lists or other personal data from your device

1.2. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our Service, we collect the following minimal technical information:

  • Device Identifier: An anonymous ID (IDFV for iOS) to manage your energy balance and purchases.
  • Face Data (Transient): When you upload a photo for AI processing, the app identifies facial areas to apply filters. This data is non-persistent (see Section 3.8).
  • Usage Data: Anonymous statistics like number of generations and app version.
  • Purchase Information: Managed via RevenueCat (Apple App Store).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide AI-driven image and video generation
  • To maintain your energy balance and purchase history
  • To ensure content safety and prevent the generation of prohibited content
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Services and Data Handling

3.1. fal.ai (AI Processing)

To enable AI features, we use fal.ai. When you request a generation:

  • Your input image is securely transmitted to fal.ai via encrypted HTTPS.
  • The data is processed in volatile memory to generate the output.
  • Retention: fal.ai does not store your photos or facial data after the generation is complete.

3.8. Face Data Disclosure (Apple Guideline 2.1)

What face data does the app collect?
The app processes photos provided by the user to detect facial landmarks and features using AI models. This is not used for biometric identification.

Purpose of collection:
This data is used exclusively to apply artistic AI transformations, such as changing style, clothing, or environment in the generated output.

Sharing and Third Parties:
Images containing face data are shared with our processing provider, fal.ai. We do not share this data with any other third parties or marketing firms.

Storage and Retention:
Face data is transient. It is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers or by our partners after the generation is finished (usually within 30 seconds).

Security:
All data transmission is protected by industry-standard HTTPS encryption.

10. Content Safety and Prohibited Content

The App strictly adheres to safety guidelines. We use automated filters (Safety Checkers) to prevent the generation of objectionable content. The following is strictly prohibited:

  • NSFW and Nudity: Any attempts to generate sexually explicit content or the removal of clothing from persons in photos/videos.
  • Harmful Content: Depiction of violence, hate speech, or illegal activities.
  • Privacy Violations: Creating non-consensual content of other individuals.

We reserve the right to block users who attempt to bypass these safety filters.
